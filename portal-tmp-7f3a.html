Brand peel standards · as per OVN master sheet

BrandDry ≥Wet ≥UnitTrials on filePass rate

PASS = lab average ≥ standard. CONSIDER = below standard but failure mode is PU tear (adhesive stronger than foam; OVN notes "passed due to PU transferred to fabric"). FAIL = adhesive failure below standard. Standards are compared numerically in the unit OVN records on the sheet; lab averages are reported in kgf/cm.

How the set-point is built
Tpeak = max( Tphysics , Tvalidated ) · rounded up to 5 °C · 145 ≤ T ≤ 180

1 · Physics minimum. 5029F sits at the fabric/foam interface and is heated through the fabric by the top belt. Interface temperature follows T(t) = Tbelt − (Tbelt − Tin)·e−t/τ; it must sit at ≥ 130 °C (TDS advised application temperature) for ≥ 8 s to flow and wet the foam. τ = τ0 + 0.5·min(thickness, 2√(α·dwell)) with α = 0.83 mm²/s for PU foam; τ0 is fitted so that the lowest validated OVN pass (NIKE.01 · 145 °C · 3.5 m/min · 4.2 mm) sits exactly on the limit. Dwell = 2.0 m heating zone ÷ speed; four 0.5 m zones, 12 mould groups.

2 · Validated recipe. Each OVN trial is scored for similarity to your recipe (density, thickness, fabric weight, speed, brand). The nearest PASS trial's zone temperature is adopted if it is above the physics minimum. Trials marked CONSIDER are used for strength prediction but never as a temperature floor.

3 · Predicted peel. Distance-weighted average of the 4 nearest trials, corrected −6 %/°C below and +3 %/°C above the trial temperature up to 165 °C (saturation: beyond 165 °C the failure mode is PU tear and the number is set by foam density, not by the adhesive). Verdict against the brand threshold from the OVN master sheet.

Limits. Floor 145 °C (lowest validated). 180 °C = Q8 mould ceiling (catalogue). Above 170 °C with 0.085D Ultra foam: scorch and strike-through risk on light linings (< 200 g/m²). Gap 0 mm and 0.30–0.40 MPa are the OVN standard window; 15 °C chiller. Pre-application trials remain essential (ALC TDS).
